flyBy privacy

flyBy is a phone game. There is no player account and no sign-in. The game does not ask for a name, email, or location to play.

A best score may be stored on the device only.

The sky theme and bird character are free. Optional Apple in-app purchases (non-consumable): Night ($0.99), Space ($0.99), UFO character ($0.99), Space+UFO bundle ($1.49), No Ads ($1.99), and Complete Set Space or UFO upgrades ($0.49 when you already own one half). Apple handles the payment. flyBy does not get a card number. Product IDs: com.playflyby.game.theme.night, com.playflyby.game.theme.space, com.playflyby.game.character.ufo, com.playflyby.game.bundle.spaceufo, com.playflyby.game.adfree, com.playflyby.game.upgrade.ufo, and com.playflyby.game.upgrade.space.

No ads run during a flight, on launch, or as a banner. After a flight ends, continue can show a rewarded video. A completed view grants flights per app rules. Closing the ad early grants nothing. If no ad is ready, play still continues.

Rewarded ads are Unity Ads. They are not Google, not AdMob, and not AdSense. Unity may receive device and ad-interaction data needed to show and measure that ad. flyBy does not require tracking to play. Declining any tracking prompt still lets you play.

Support: https://playflyby.com/support and +1 352-325-7735. To report an inappropriate ad, use Report ad on the death screen in the app.

flyBy does not sell personal information.

This policy covers the flyBy iOS app and https://playflyby.com.
