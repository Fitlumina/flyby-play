flyBy privacy

flyBy is a phone game. There is no player account and no sign-in. The game does not ask for a name, email, or location to play.

A best score may be stored on the device only.

The sky theme is free. Night theme and no-ads are optional Apple in-app purchases (non-consumable). Apple handles the payment. flyBy does not get a card number. Product IDs: com.playflyby.game.theme.night and com.playflyby.game.adfree. Prices are on Apple’s sheet, not hardcoded here.

No ads run during a flight, on launch, or as a banner. After a flight ends, tapping start again can show a rewarded video. A completed view starts a new flight. Closing the ad early grants nothing. If no ad is ready, a new flight still starts.

Rewarded ads are Unity Ads. They are not Google, not AdMob, and not AdSense. Unity may receive device and ad-interaction data needed to show and measure that ad. flyBy does not require tracking to play. Declining any tracking prompt still lets you play.

To report an inappropriate ad, use Report ad on the death screen in the app. That in-app control is the contact path. There is no support email for flyBy.

flyBy does not sell personal information.

This policy covers the flyBy iOS app and https://playflyby.com.
