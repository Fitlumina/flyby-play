flyBy

flyBy is a phone game. There is no player account and no sign-in.

How to get help: after a flight, on the death screen, tap Report ad. That in-app control is the contact path. There is no support email for flyBy.

Privacy policy: https://playflyby.com/privacy.

Restore purchases is in the store inside the app.

Night and no-ads are Apple purchases.
